EEG decoding in your browser

Open-source tools for making sense of EEG, the electrical activity of the brain. Everything runs in your browser, so your recordings never leave your computer.

Principles

  • In the browser Private by design. No install and no account.
  • Open source Anyone can read the code, check it and improve it.
  • Visual Clear plots that make brain signals easy to read.

Named after Michael Ventris, who decoded Linear B, a script nobody could read, by finding the patterns in it.

These are research tools, not medical devices.
